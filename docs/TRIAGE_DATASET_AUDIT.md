# FedMML-ED-Triage Dataset Audit

## 1. Overview
This audit evaluates the integrity, diversity, and safety of the `fedmml_ed_triage_dataset.csv` before attempting to train any ML triage model on it.

- **Dataset Path:** `C:\MahaArogya\data\raw\fedmml_ed_triage\fedmml_ed_triage_dataset.csv`
- **Total Rows:** 87,234
- **Unique Encounters:** 87,234

## 2. Patient & Diversity Metrics
- **Unique Patient IDs:** **96**
- **Unique Chief Complaints (Text):** **28**

**Analysis:** This represents a catastrophic lack of diversity. The 87,234 rows are mathematically derived from only 96 unique human baselines and exactly 28 unique text strings. On average, there are **908 rows per patient**, generated by synthetically perturbing the vitals of the original 96 patients.

## 3. Label Distribution (ESI Level)
- ESI 3 (Priority): 41,389 (47.4%)
- ESI 4 (Routine): 23,199 (26.6%)
- ESI 2 (Urgent): 16,789 (19.2%)
- ESI 5 (Routine): 4,933 (5.7%)
- ESI 1 (Emergency): 924 (1.1%)

## 4. Leakage and Duplication
- **Exact Row Duplicates:** 0
- **Text Duplicates (Near Duplicates):** 87,206 (99.96% of the text data is duplicated).
- **Patient-Level Leakage:** The previous model used a `patient_id_grouped` split (67 train / 14 val / 15 test). While this technically prevents the exact same *patient ID* from appearing in both train and test, the fact that there are only **28 unique Chief Complaints** across all 96 patients means the text inputs in the test set perfectly overlap with the text inputs in the training set. 

## 5. Why the Previous Model Failed
The previous `MultimodalTriageClassifier` achieved an artificial 100% accuracy (`val_accuracy: 1.0`, `emergency_recall: 1.0`) because of **Dataset Memorization**.
1. The text backbone (MuRIL) memorized the 28 unique text strings.
2. The tabular MLP memorized the specific vital sign ranges assigned to those strings.
3. The model learned to act as a 28-item lookup table rather than generalizing clinical triage rules.

## 6. Final Verdict
**STATUS: UNSAFE FOR TRAINING.**

This dataset is a highly synthetic, low-variance artifact. It does not contain real-world diversity. Training a neural network on 28 unique text strings and 96 patients to make emergency medical triage decisions is extraordinarily dangerous and will completely fail on novel real-world inputs.

**Recommendation:** 
Do **NOT** proceed to model training with `fedmml_ed_triage`. We must source a larger, non-synthetic, diverse dataset, or we must heavily restrict ML triage to non-emergency prioritization while maintaining the deterministic `SafetyRuleEngine` as the absolute authority.
